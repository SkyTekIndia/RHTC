1. ANM Course

Rural Health Training Centre Najafgarh conduct combined entrance exam every year for 80+8 seats each of RHTC Najafgarh & Lady Reading Health School Bara Hindu Rao, New Delhi as per guidelines of Delhi Nursing Council / Indian Nursing Council with advertisement in the two National daily NEWS PAPERS namely Danik Jagran in Hindi & the Times of India in English published by DAVP, Delhi along with terms & conditions & proforma attached.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR FILLING ONLINE APPLICATION FORM

ANM Online Application is spread over different sections designed to obtain information related to the candidate’s Personal Profile, Educational Qualification, Image, and Documents, Fee payment and Submit Application.

Before filling up the ANM Online Application, candidates are advised to go through the relevant Advertisement available on www.rhtcnajafgarh.in

A. PROCEDURE AND STEPS TO BE FOLLOWED TO SUBMIT ONLINE APPLICATION FORM

The Application will be submitted online through the URL https://rhtcnajafrarh.in (Tentative Online submission of application shall start from 8th July. 2022 to 10th Aug. 2022 up to 12:00 Midnight)

STEP-1: Click on the “Apply Application for ANM Registration” button fill-up the Registration Form.
STEP-2: After submission of ANM Registration Form and payment, take printout of Application Form. Payment confirmation mail also send to your registered mail id. In the mail and printed Registration Form you get Application ID. Use Application ID to download Admit Card from Website. Admit Card dates will be update later. Check regular updates on website.
STEP-3: Keep ready following documents before starting to fill the online Application Form:
  • Profile image (Candidates recent photograph) (upto 200 KB)
  • Signature image (upto 200 KB)
  • Aadhar Card
  • 10th Mark Sheet
  • 12th Mark sheet
  • Candidate Character Certificate (Not more than 6 month old)/ Domicile Certificate
  • SC/ST/OBC/PH/EWSs Certificate as per Govt. of India format (Central Govt. format only)
  • 12th Certificate/ 12th Admit Card

Note- The required/ mandatory documents should be uploaded in a single file in .jpeg, .jpg, .pdf or .png format with size not exceeding 500 KBs.
